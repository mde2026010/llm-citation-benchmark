# Verified Research Papers

The 20 entries below were checked against authoritative publisher/proceedings/arXiv records during preparation of this repository. The links are provided for verification and further reading.

## Citation fabrication and accuracy

1. **Fabrication and errors in the bibliographic citations generated by ChatGPT**  
   William H. Walters; Esther Isabelle Wilder, 2023. *Scientific Reports*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5)  
   **Why relevant:** Core empirical study of fabricated and inaccurate bibliographic citations from GPT-3.5 and GPT-4.

2. **Evaluation of Large Language Model Performance and Reliability for Citations and References in Scholarly Writing: Cross-Disciplinary Study**  
   Joseph Mugaanyi; Liuying Cai; Sumei Cheng; Caide Lu; Jing Huang, 2024. *Journal of Medical Internet Research*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e52935)  
   **Why relevant:** Compares citation/reference accuracy across natural-science and humanities topics.

3. **Learning to Fake It: Limited Responses and Fabricated References Provided by ChatGPT for Medical Questions**  
   Jocelyn Gravel; Madeleine D’Amours-Gravel; Esli Osmanlliu, 2023. *Mayo Clinic Proceedings: Digital Health*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mcpdig.2023.05.004)  
   **Why relevant:** Reports fabricated references produced for medical questions.

4. **High Rates of Fabricated and Inaccurate References in ChatGPT-Generated Medical Content**  
   Mehul Bhattacharyya; Valerie M. Miller; Debjani Bhattacharyya; Larry E. Miller, 2023. *Cureus*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.39238)  
   **Why relevant:** Measures authenticity and field-level errors in ChatGPT-generated medical references.

5. **Hallucination Rates and Reference Accuracy of ChatGPT and Bard for Systematic Reviews: Comparative Analysis**  
   Mikaël Chelli; Jules Descamps; Vincent Lavoué; et al., 2024. *Journal of Medical Internet Research*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e53164)  
   **Why relevant:** Compares GPT-3.5, GPT-4 and Bard reference retrieval/hallucination rates.

6. **This Reference Does Not Exist: An Exploration of LLM Citation Accuracy and Relevance**  
   Byun et al., 2024. *HCI International / HCINLP*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2024.hcinlp-1.3/)  
   **Why relevant:** Studies title/author accuracy and relevance of model-recommended citations.

7. **ChatGPT as Research Scientist: Probing GPT’s capabilities as a Research Librarian, Research Ethicist, Data Generator, and Data Predictor**  
   Authors as listed by PNAS, 2024. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2404328121)  
   **Why relevant:** Includes a controlled analysis of hallucinated and erroneous references.

## Citation generation

8. **Content-Based Citation Recommendation**  
   Chandra Bhagavatula; Sergey Feldman; Russell Power; Waleed Ammar, 2018. *NAACL-HLT*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/N18-1022/)  
   **Why relevant:** Foundational citation-recommendation work using document representations and reranking.

9. **Controllable Citation Sentence Generation with Language Models**  
   Nianlong Gu; Richard Hahnloser, 2024. *ACL Workshop on Scholarly Document Processing*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2024.sdp-1.4/)  
   **Why relevant:** Studies controllable generation of citation sentences and citation intent.

10. **Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations**  
   Tianyu Gao; Howard Yen; Jiatong Yu; Danqi Chen, 2023. *EMNLP*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/)  
   **Why relevant:** Introduces ALCE, a benchmark for automatic evaluation of answers with citations.

## Citation-supported generation

11. **Citation-Enhanced Generation for LLM-based Chatbots**  
   Weitao Li; Junkai Li; Weizhi Ma; Yang Liu, 2024. *ACL*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.79/)  
   **Why relevant:** Post-hoc retrieval and citation generation to improve support for generated statements.

12. **Learning to Generate Answers with Citations via Factual Consistency Models**  
   Rami Aly; Zhiqiang Tang; Samson Tan; George Karypis, 2024. *ACL*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.641/)  
   **Why relevant:** Uses factual-consistency models to improve citation-supported answer generation.

## Hallucination evaluation

13. **SelfCheckGPT: Zero-Resource Black-Box Hallucination Detection for Generative Large Language Models**  
   Potsawee Manakul; Adian Liusie; Mark Gales, 2023. *EMNLP*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.557/)  
   **Why relevant:** Sampling-based black-box hallucination detection.

14. **HaluEval: A Large-Scale Hallucination Evaluation Benchmark for Large Language Models**  
   Junyi Li; Xiaoxue Cheng; Xin Zhao; Jian-Yun Nie; Ji-Rong Wen, 2023. *EMNLP*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.397/)  
   **Why relevant:** Large-scale benchmark for recognizing hallucinated content.

15. **FActScore: Fine-grained Atomic Evaluation of Factual Precision in Long Form Text Generation**  
   Sewon Min; Kalpesh Krishna; Xinxi Lyu; et al., 2023. *EMNLP*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.741/)  
   **Why relevant:** Evaluates factual precision at the atomic-fact level using evidence retrieval.

16. **HalluLens: LLM Hallucination Benchmark**  
   Yejin Bang; Ziwei Ji; Alan Schelten; et al., 2025. *ACL*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1176/)  
   **Why relevant:** Benchmark and taxonomy for intrinsic/extrinsic hallucination.

17. **HALoGEN: Fantastic LLM Hallucinations and Where to Find Them**  
   Abhilasha Ravichander; Shrusti Ghela; David Wadden; Yejin Choi, 2025. *ACL*.  
   [Paper / DOI](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.71/)  
   **Why relevant:** Large hallucination benchmark with high-precision verification across domains.
