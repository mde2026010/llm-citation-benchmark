# Curated Research Papers

These 20 scholarly resources are drawn directly from the submitted paper's bibliography. Each was independently checked against a primary scholarly or official record. The repository also records two journalism studies and one court case separately in the citation audit because they are important evidence sources but are not scholarly papers.

## Foundational citation fabrication

- **Artificial Hallucinations in ChatGPT: Implications in Scientific Writing** — Hussam Alkaissi; Samy I. McFarlane, 2023, *Cureus*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.35179). **Why relevant:** early medical/scientific-writing evidence of ChatGPT reference hallucination.

- **Exploring the Boundaries of Reality: Investigating the Phenomenon of Artificial Intelligence Hallucination in Scientific Writing Through ChatGPT References** — Sai Anirudh Athaluri; Sandeep Varma Manthena; V. S. R. Krishna Manoj Kesapragada; et al., 2023, *Cureus*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.37432). **Why relevant:** large early audit of 178 ChatGPT-generated research-proposal references.

- **Fabrication and Errors in the Bibliographic Citations Generated by ChatGPT** — William H. Walters; Esther Isabelle Wilder, 2023, *Scientific Reports*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5). **Why relevant:** foundational empirical measurement of fabricated ChatGPT citations across 42 topics.

- **Do Language Models Know When They're Hallucinating References?** — Ayush Agrawal; Mirac Suzgun; Lester Mackey; Adam Tauman Kalai, 2024, *Findings of ACL: EACL 2024*. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-eacl.62/). **Why relevant:** tests whether models can internally detect hallucinated references through consistency checks.

## Benchmarks, taxonomies and citation-grounded generation

- **HalluLens: LLM Hallucination Benchmark** — Yejin Bang; Ziwei Ji; Alan Schelten; et al., 2025, *ACL 2025*. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1176/). **Why relevant:** provides a modern hallucination taxonomy and dynamically generated benchmark.

- **Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations** — Tianyu Gao; Howard Yen; Jiatong Yu; Danqi Chen, 2023, *EMNLP 2023*. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/). **Why relevant:** introduces ALCE for automatic evaluation of answers with citations.

- **A Survey on Hallucination in Large Language Models: Principles, Taxonomy, Challenges, and Open Questions** — Lei Huang; Weijiang Yu; Weitao Ma; et al., 2025, *ACM Transactions on Information Systems*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1145/3703155). **Why relevant:** surveys hallucination definitions, taxonomies, detection and mitigation.

- **Evaluating Verifiability in Generative Search Engines** — Nelson F. Liu; Tianyi Zhang; Percy Liang, 2023, *Findings of ACL: EMNLP 2023*. [Primary record](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-emnlp.467/). **Why relevant:** measures citation precision and citation recall in generative search engines.

- **Reference Hallucination Score for Medical Artificial Intelligence Chatbots: Development and Usability Study** — Fahad Aljamaan; Mohammed-Hani Temsah; Ibrahim Altamimi; et al., 2024, *JMIR Medical Informatics*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.2196/54345). **Why relevant:** operationalizes bibliographic hallucination with a multi-field score.

## Cross-model and domain-specific empirical studies

- **Assessing the Performance of 8 AI Chatbots in Bibliographic Reference Retrieval: Grok and DeepSeek Outperform ChatGPT, but None Are Fully Accurate** — Álvaro Cabezas-Clavijo; Pavel Sidorenko-Bautista, 2025, *arXiv*. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18059). **Why relevant:** compares eight chatbots on academic bibliographic reference retrieval.

- **Hallucination Rates and Reference Accuracy of ChatGPT and Bard for Systematic Reviews: Comparative Analysis** — Mikaël Chelli; Jules Descamps; Vincent Lavoué; et al., 2024, *Journal of Medical Internet Research*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.2196/53164). **Why relevant:** replicates systematic-review reference generation with ChatGPT and Bard.

- **Influence of Topic Familiarity and Prompt Specificity on Citation Fabrication in Mental Health Research Using Large Language Models: Experimental Study** — Jonathan Linardon; Hannah K. Jarman; Zachary McClure; et al., 2025, *JMIR Mental Health*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.2196/80371). **Why relevant:** isolates topic familiarity and prompt specificity as factors in citation fabrication.

- **Reference Hallucination in AI-Assisted Academic Writing: A Comparative Analysis of ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity in Rotator Cuff Literature** — Claimed in paper as: Resmi Rajalekshmi & Devendra K. Agrawal, 2026, *Indian Journal of Orthopaedics*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43465-026-01807-0). **Why relevant:** tests ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity on 3,150 rotator-cuff references and RHS.

## Legal and agentic/deep-research reliability

- **LegalCiteBench: Evaluating Citation Reliability in Legal Language Models** — Sijia Chen; Hang Yin; Shunfan Zhou, 2026, *arXiv*. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10186). **Why relevant:** benchmarks closed-book legal citation retrieval and verification.

- **Large Legal Fictions: Profiling Legal Hallucinations in Large Language Models** — Matthew Dahl; Varun Magesh; Mirac Suzgun; Daniel E. Ho, 2024, *Journal of Legal Analysis*. [Primary record](https://doi.org/10.1093/jla/laae003). **Why relevant:** profiles legal hallucinations in large language models.

- **Detecting and Correcting Reference Hallucinations in Commercial LLMs and Deep Research Agents** — Delip Rao; Eric Wong; Chris Callison-Burch, 2026, *arXiv*. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03173). **Why relevant:** measures citation URL hallucination in commercial LLMs and deep-research agents.

- **GhostCite: A Large-Scale Analysis of Citation Validity in the Age of Large Language Models** — Zuyao Xu; Yuqi Qiu; Lu Sun; et al., 2026, *arXiv*. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06718). **Why relevant:** large-scale LLM citation-validity study and CiteVerifier framework.

- **Hallucination-Free? Assessing the Reliability of Leading AI Legal Research Tools** — Varun Magesh; Faiz Surani; Matthew Dahl; et al., 2024, *arXiv*. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20362). **Why relevant:** preregistered evaluation of RAG-grounded legal research tools.

## Large-scale / authority effects

- **Authority, Truth, and Citation Bias: A Large-Scale Multi-Domain Benchmark for Studying Epistemic Susceptibility in Large Language Models** — Aryan Khurana; Aravind Ramana RN; Dhruv Kumar, 2026, *arXiv*. [Primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13104). **Why relevant:** isolates citation-based authority effects across truth/citation conditions.
