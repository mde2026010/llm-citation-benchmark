# Datasets and Benchmarks

## 1. ALCE — Automatic LLMs' Citation Evaluation

- **Type:** Citation-supported answer benchmark
- **Purpose:** Evaluate fluency, answer correctness, and citation quality.
- **Research use:** Directly relevant to measuring whether generated answers are adequately supported by cited evidence.
- **Source:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/

## 2. DeepResearch Bench

- **Type:** Deep-research-agent benchmark
- **Purpose:** Evaluate research quality and information retrieval/collection, including citation accuracy.
- **Research use:** Directly relevant to comparing agentic research systems.
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11763/
- **Code/data:** https://github.com/Ayanami0730/deep_research_bench

## 3. DRBench — A Realistic Benchmark for Enterprise Deep Research

- **Type:** Enterprise deep-research benchmark
- **Purpose:** Multi-step research using public and private information.
- **Research use:** Useful for understanding how citation/factuality evaluation changes in agentic workflows.
- **Source:** https://openreview.net/forum?id=IGYQ4c92e2
- **Code:** https://github.com/ServiceNow/drbench

## 4. BrowseComp

- **Type:** Web-browsing-agent benchmark
- **Purpose:** Evaluate agents on difficult, entangled information-search problems.
- **Research use:** Provides a benchmark context for comparing web research capability with citation reliability.
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12516
- **Code:** https://github.com/openai/simple-evals

## 5. HaluEval

- **Type:** Hallucination evaluation benchmark
- **Purpose:** Evaluate recognition of hallucinated content.
- **Research use:** Useful background for broader hallucination measurement.
- **Source:** https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.397/

## 6. HalluLens

- **Type:** LLM hallucination benchmark
- **Purpose:** Evaluate intrinsic and extrinsic hallucination using a unified taxonomy.
- **Research use:** Useful for defining and separating hallucination types.
- **Source:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1176/

## 7. HALoGEN

- **Type:** Large hallucination benchmark
- **Purpose:** Evaluate factual hallucinations across many domains and models.
- **Research use:** Useful for designing scalable verification pipelines.
- **Source:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.71/
